This Round's Confirmation Points

Use Word-style page-corner marks: the arms stay outside the text area, and the four L-shaped tips face inward. Click “Show” at the top to see the text boundary align exactly with the tips.

01 · All four tips face inwardTop-left ┘, top-right └, bottom-left ┐, bottom-right ┌. Each L-shaped tip points toward the body text, and the arm extends from the tip toward the page edge.
02 · Tips define the boundaryThe four tips define the text-layout rectangle. The corner marks themselves stay outside it, so the body text is not inset a second time.
03 · Text aligns directly to the boundaryThe usable text area starts directly at the boundary defined by the four tips, with no extra horizontal or vertical deduction for the mark length.
04 · Word-style paginationOnce content reaches the lower-corner boundary, the next block moves to the next page; the page gap is not editable.
