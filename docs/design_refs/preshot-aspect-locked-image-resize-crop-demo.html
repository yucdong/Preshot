Design notes
01Side handles only
No top, bottom, or corner resize controls, reducing accidental ratio changes.
02Live group preview
Animation-frame updates preserve source order and fixed 12 px gaps; items wrap before overflow or intersection, then commit on release.
03Stable smart guides
Equal width and height take priority, followed by group and image edges. Snap entry is 6 px and release is 10 px, with magenta guides or compact brackets.
04Unified, bar-free layout
Confirmed crops fill their true ratio without black bars. Crops, window changes, and resize commits share one wrapping engine, and group height follows row count.
