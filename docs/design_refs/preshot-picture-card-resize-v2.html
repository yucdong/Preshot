Interaction Changes in This Round

Click and drag directly in the middle canvas to validate the behavior. Transparent hit areas show only light feedback on hover.

Eight-direction resizing

Both the image card and individual images support single-axis resizing from all four edges and simultaneous width/height resizing from all four corners.

Non-text cursors

Images use a pointer cursor, the gray image-group area uses a grab cursor, and resize hit areas use the matching resize cursor.

Images use a pointer cursor

Show a pointer cursor when the mouse enters image content; switch to a dragging state after press-and-move.

Balanced image resize hit areas

Use 20px inner hit bands on all four edges and 24×24px corners to preserve usability while reducing accidental resize actions.

Resize on hover

No pre-click selection is required; edges and corners become active as soon as the pointer enters the image, and selection happens automatically when dragging starts.

Opposite edge stays fixed

When dragging the top edge downward to shrink, the top moves down while the bottom stays fixed; the left edge and corners likewise keep the opposite edge or corner fixed without forcing top alignment.

Compact top-right toolbar

Keep it 30px tall and pinned to the top-right; do not include size values, +/- controls, or a restore-size button.

Intra-group and cross-group drag-and-drop

Press and drag an image to reorder it; when moving into another image group, show a live insertion placeholder and complete the move on release.

Reorder in document flow

Drag the gray empty area of an image group to move the whole group above or below text blocks or other components.

All four edges are resizable

Reserve 24px of usable space at the top; when dragging the top edge upward, the top moves up, the bottom stays fixed, and the group frame grows taller.

Flicker-free resizing

While dragging, update only the lightweight preview frame so the image content stays stable; commit the final size once on release and reflow only once.
